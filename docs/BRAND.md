# Trench Coat brand system - Velvet Collar

**Version:** 1.1.0 | **Codename:** Velvet Collar  
**Tagline:** The shadows are your ally.  
**Positioning:** Legal-first multi-hop privacy cloak. Serious operational security with hand-crafted atmospheric beauty. Not a crime toolkit. Not neon cyberpunk.

## Philosophy

Film-noir illustration meets soft watercolor. Quiet power, protective elegance, rain and fog, ink linework on deep indigo paper. Sibling sites intentionally use different languages; Trench Coat owns noir watercolor.
